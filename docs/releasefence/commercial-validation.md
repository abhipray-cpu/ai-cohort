# Commercial hypothesis and design-partner plan

The buyer pays for a release-risk workflow, not a third copy of their trace data.
Competitive differentiation and willingness to pay remain unvalidated.

## Discovery before expanding scope

Interview 8–12 qualified teams as a proposed target, not an accomplished result.
Ask for their last actual agent incident and the evidence they already keep:
release manifest, alert, trace, rollback command, runtime version and incident end.
Separate diagnosis time, mitigation time, false pages, exposure and review effort.
Do not collect private payloads during a sales discussion without explicit rights.

Ask who owns production credentials, which changes require approval, why existing
LangSmith/Arize/Datadog/feature flags are insufficient, and what an integration
would have to demonstrate to earn a paid pilot. Record disconfirming answers.

## Pilot hypothesis

Test an assisted $500–$1,500/month pilot for one application and two environments,
with explicit event/retention/notification limits and pass-through vendor/inference
costs disclosed. This range is a hypothesis, not a supported market price or quote.
Manual contracting/invoicing can precede self-serve billing; no payment integration
is promised in the first engineering scope.

Proposed next checkpoint: three qualified design partners, at least one paid
bounded staging pilot, and a reviewed comparison with the customer's current
workflow. No outreach or contracting is authorized by the documentation task.

Pilot success criteria must be agreed before installing the connector: acceptable
onboarding effort, known-fault detection, acceptable false-alert burden, successful
supported rollback drill, and reduced evidence-assembly effort. Report before/after
workload differences; do not claim causal ROI from one handpicked incident.

## Stop or reconsider

- Teams need only cheap dashboards already available elsewhere.
- Their existing platform solves the complete workflow satisfactorily.
- Every pilot requires a bespoke infrastructure adapter without reusable demand.
- Buyers will not pay or even permit a scoped staging integration.
- False positives/control-plane overhead outweigh the saved operator effort.

No uptime SLA, security certification, guaranteed savings, all-infrastructure
support or trademark clearance is implied by the proposed paid pilot.
