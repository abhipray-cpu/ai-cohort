# Research and source register

Checked 22 October 2026 using official web pages. Initial source discovery below
has now been followed by a bounded [actual-source checkpoint](real-data-validation-2026-10-22.md)
and [free Jev research](jev-free-access-research.md). Twelve programme PDFs were
downloaded; eleven parsed. This is not complete market research, rights clearance,
consumer invoice evidence, actual provider inference or a claim result.

## Primary sources

| ID | Source | Intended evidence and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | [NHTSA datasets/APIs](https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis) | Public recall and related record acquisition. Year/make/model candidates are not vehicle-specific open-recall confirmation. Endpoint availability/freshness must be captured per run. |

Independent specialist interpretation and actual vehicle applicability remain unverified; no current claim window inferred. |

Maintain source IDs and canonical URLs, acquisition/publication/effective dates,
HTTP metadata, exact bytes/hash, page map, parser versions, access/redistribution
notes and reviewer. Absence of a licensing statement is not unrestricted reuse.
Do not commit private records or redistribute whole manufacturer PDFs without
reviewed rights. Store references and minimal approved excerpts where appropriate.

## What is established versus hypothesized

Established: official-source infrastructure exists for a bounded discovery corpus;
vehicle attributes and manufacturer communications have distinct purposes. Public
sources do not supply complete consumer evidence, live VIN applicability or refunds.

Hypotheses: consumers will pay for evidence preparation; discovery beyond recall
lookup is valuable; document completeness improves case progress; the retained
history/monitoring experience is useful. None has been measured. No statement that
RepairClaim is novel, unsaturated, cheaper, more accurate or endorsed by a regulator.

## Competitor and demand research still required

Compare official manufacturer portals, consumer advocacy services, repair-quote
assistants and generic chat/document tools using the same case. Review first-party
features/pricing/data practices before claiming gaps. Run at least 12 consented
consumer interviews and three automotive consumer-assistance/specialist interviews;
record actual alternatives, trust objections, document willingness, usefulness and
price response. These are research targets, not completed interviews or conversion
statistics. Do not recruit by implying guaranteed recovery.

## Capability research is separate from product authority

Prior [Jev 1.14 release research](https://typesafe.ai/blog/jev-1-14) found optional
decision/experimental visual capabilities. They do not justify a coverage decision
or a requirement. If evaluated later, pin the model ID, check the
[model reference](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models), resolve alias discrepancies and
respect [experimental-image warnings](https://docs.typesafe.ai/images).
No account, key, installation, external-provider exception or live inference is
authorized merely by this source register. The product design remains replaceable
at the decision/generation boundary.
