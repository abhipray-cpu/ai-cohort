# Actual data and independent evaluation plan

**Initial bounded public-source validation is measured in the
[22 October checkpoint](real-data-validation-2026-10-22.md): 12 downloads, 11 parsed
documents, 48 authored boundary cases through actual HTTP. No independently
labelled dataset or private consumer pilot has been completed.** Public source existence
does not establish usable volume, label quality, complete consumer evidence or
successful recovery. Distinguish the following tracks in every report.

Latest measured additions: [free-proxy authored smoke](jevmodel-validation-2026-10-24.md)
and [400 actual public complaint metadata workflows](public-complaint-validation-2026-10-24.md).
The latter uses only four distinct minimized vehicle inputs; it does not establish
400 independent semantic cases, narrative accuracy or genuine invoice completeness.
The user authorized this separate public-data track; four bounded official queries
were reacquired after a tested field-name correction. No private invoices or raw
complaint narratives were retained or sent externally. The original pilot/label
targets below are unchanged.

## Evidence tracks

| Track | Inputs | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public-source study | Actual official programme documents and metadata | Acquisition, parsing, conditions, provenance and candidate matching | Complete personal eligibility, workshop facts or reimbursement |
| Public consumer-report metadata | Official, unverified complaint vehicle identities, minimized before storage/egress | Scope and missing-evidence handling through actual workflows | Narrative interpretation, repair dates, paid repairs, invoices or independent eligibility accuracy |
| Authored/adversarial mechanics | Clearly labelled invoices/facts/responses and fault injection | Deterministic boundaries, UI, privacy, retry/recovery and condition logic | Actual customer data or manufacturer outcomes |
| Consented private pilot | Real estimates/invoices, payments and correspondence with permission | Actual end-to-end case preparation/usability and observed responses | Universal entitlement or guaranteed recovery |
| Deployment drill | Target Windows host and protected hostname | Resource, identity, tunnel, restart and restore behaviour | Domain accuracy or business viability |

## Acquisition and corpus freeze

Use the [source register](research-and-sources.md). Operator-reviewed acquisition
proposal: at most 120 documents and 250 MiB from official registered sources, no
consumer records or automated VIN enumeration. The explicit acquisition harness
now implements these bounds; the initial 12-document run stayed within them.
Future expansion still needs legitimate sources and must preserve failures. Respect licenses,
terms/rate limits and quarantine unreadable, duplicate or unsupported material.

Target at least 100 distinct public documents across at least 20 reviewed programme
families, including coverage-positive material, bulletin-only hard negatives,
historical/expired terms, revisions, reimbursement instructions and configuration
restrictions. Exact availability for the narrow Honda scope is unknown. If volume
cannot be acquired legitimately, report the inventory shortfall and obtain an
explicit scope/gate decision; do not invent documents, duplicate revisions as new
families, silently add brands or lower the target and call it rigorous validation.

Manifest: URL/final URL, source ID, fetched/publication/effective dates when known,
HTTP result, SHA-256, byte/page counts, rights/access notes, parser/OCR version,
programme family/revision, jurisdiction, status and exclusion reason. Freeze raw,
extracted and registry interpretation separately. Private raw records never enter
git. Public documents are not automatically cleared for redistribution.

## Labels and splits

Create at least 300 independently reviewed repair-description/programme pairs,
including at least 100 hard-negative pairs. Pair descriptions derived from sources
or authored by reviewers are labelled as such, not called real invoices. Cover wrong
component, wrong model/configuration, dates/mileage boundaries, missing evidence,
contradictions, service bulletin without coverage, source outages and stale revisions.

Group by programme family and related vehicle/component family; keep at least 60
pairs in a locked holdout with multiple programme families. No near-duplicate family
revisions in both tuning and holdout. Freeze split/prompts/rules/thresholds before
holdout inference. Runtime retrieval may access the relevant unlabeled source
documents, as intended for a grounded product; it may not access evaluator verdicts,
expected conditions, suggested drafts or gold files. Independently review disputed
labels and record disagreements, reviewer qualifications and adjudication.

Reviewed registry conditions are product knowledge, not hidden ground truth. Test
raw-document extraction against independent condition annotations separately from
case evaluation against a reviewed registry. Do not claim end-to-end automated
condition understanding after supplying gold-normalized conditions as input.

## Comparison and metrics

Compare a reviewed deterministic registry/keyword baseline with the full retrieval/
casework workflow using the same source freeze, cases and finite allowances. If a
decision provider such as Jev is later selected, add a separate with/without arm,
pin version and report actual benefit or lack of benefit. No provider is included
merely to satisfy a marketing diagram.

Measure separately: acquisition success and exclusions; field extraction correctness;
condition completeness; candidate recall/precision; hard-negative false leads;
citation existence and contextual support; unsupported factual assertions; correct
unknown/conflict handling; next-question utility; packet completeness; user effort;
source-change sensitivity and notification false positives. Report denominators,
families, per-stratum results and uncertainty intervals. Bootstrap/group intervals
by family where appropriate; pair count is not independent family count.

For execution, report failed/timeouts/cancelled/partial/manual cases, retries, wall
time, first useful result, observed tokens and priced-versus-unknown cost. A timeout
is not excluded from the end-to-end success denominator. Model confidence, judge
agreement, source count and test count are not independent factual accuracy.

## Finite actual-provider campaigns

Implementation and deterministic/browser checkpoints precede claimed actual-model
E2E acceptance. Proposed first smoke: six public-source-derived cases, at most 48
total paid invocations including all providers/retries. This is **not approved yet**.
Before execution obtain task-specific authority for destination/data fields, duration,
calls and unknown billing; no model/auth changes or borrowed campaign allowance.
After inspecting failures, propose a separate locked evaluation campaign with new
bounds and leave holdout uncontaminated. Mock or cache replay is not live inference.

## Consented consumer pilot

Target ten real consumer cases with permission to process estimates/invoices and
minimal supporting facts; at least five actual response/follow-up journeys where
participants choose to submit outside the app. Do not pressure users to file or
wait for a refusal to manufacture test evidence. Written pilot consent specifies
processing, retention, sharing and withdrawal. If access is absent, this gate stays
open; public complaints or fabricated PDFs cannot stand in for actual invoices.

Specialist independently reviews supported-programme interpretations and all pilot
packets for unsupported claims. Document deidentified completeness/effort/usability
outcomes; publish private material only with separate consent. Actual refunds are
optional observed outcomes, not a forced pilot target. No refunds observed means
no reimbursement-success claim. Record approvals and payments distinctly.
