## Acceptance still open

All five Day 9 mechanisms ran on actual evidence: role proposals, critic, ballots,
deterministic consensus and grounded synthesis with dissent/low confidence. This
does not establish critic recall, factual support of every inference, UK validity,
prospective usefulness or debate's benefit over baselines. Six development cases
are not a statistically sufficient held-out benchmark.

A supervised MVP still needs a connected-browser walkthrough, independent qualified
review of real drafts, and promised Windows/tunnel host acceptance. Authorized
prospective UK files are needed for prospective UK claims. Broader statistical and
paid-pilot goals remain separate from the narrower supervised MVP. Access/reviewer
questions were sent while this campaign ran; no external reviewer, browser or
Windows host was available to this execution. Those gates cannot be closed by
repeating local tests or relabelling missing evidence as a pass.
